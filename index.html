What's Included

Every visit is guided by a clear checklist, with extra attention where each service needs it most.

Standard Cleaning

Regular maintenance cleaning to keep your home fresh, tidy, and comfortable.

  • Dusting all accessible surfaces
  • Vacuuming carpets and rugs
  • Vacuuming and mopping floors
  • Sofa vacuuming
  • Kitchen surface cleaning
  • Sink and tap cleaning
  • Exterior cleaning of appliances
  • Bathroom cleaning and sanitizing
  • Toilet, shower, bathtub, and mirror cleaning
  • Emptying bins
  • General tidying
  • Change of bed linens and towels, if requested

Deep Cleaning

A more detailed and intensive cleaning focused on built-up dirt and hard-to-reach areas.

  • Everything in Standard Cleaning
  • Deep cleaning of bathrooms and kitchens, including cabinets (exterior)
  • Detailed dusting throughout all rooms
  • Cleaning skirting boards
  • Cleaning doors and door frames
  • Light switch and handle sanitizing
  • Interior window cleaning
  • Inside microwave and oven
  • Detailed vacuuming and floor care
  • Removal of built-up dirt and limescale
  • Cleaning behind and under accessible furniture
  • Bin cleaning and sanitizing

Move-In Cleaning (New Build)

Complete top-to-bottom cleaning for new-build properties before moving in.

  • Full property deep cleaning
  • Inside cabinets, drawers, and wardrobes
  • Inside oven, fridge, and appliances
  • Interior window cleaning
  • Deep bathroom sanitizing
  • Kitchen deep cleaning
  • Vacuuming and mopping all floors
  • Dust removal throughout the rooms
  • Cleaning doors, frames, skirting boards, and switches
  • Removal of construction dust

Frequently Asked Questions

Planning a house clean? Here is how our services and booking requests work.

What does standard house cleaning include?

Standard Cleaning includes dusting accessible surfaces, vacuuming, mopping, kitchen surfaces, bathroom cleaning and general tidying. See the full cleaning checklists to compare services.

Do you clean new-build homes before moving in?

Yes. Our new-build move-in service includes construction dust removal, cleaning inside cabinets and wardrobes, interior windows, kitchen appliances and deep bathroom sanitizing.

How much does house cleaning cost?

Check our prices by service and room count. The price depends on the number of bathrooms, property size and its current condition.

When can I book a cleaning appointment?

Appointment requests are available for Saturdays and Sundays, 09:00-16:00. Submit your preferred date through the booking form and send the prepared WhatsApp message. Each request is pending approval until we confirm availability.

Is Deep Cleaning currently available?

Deep Cleaning has no availability at the moment. You can request Standard Cleaning or Move-In Cleaning for a new-build home.
